# TV artwork

Created with the built-in image generation tool on 2026-10-16. Both assets are
bundled locally; displaying them makes no network or provider request. Text,
captions, QR codes, and focus indicators are rendered by the app.

- Preview: `app/src/main/res/drawable-nodpi/tv_preview_scene.png`
- Setup and dashboard: `app/src/main/res/drawable-nodpi/tv_setup_scene.png`

Asset paths below refer to the [0.23.10 release source](https://github.com/Sal7one/Hearth/tree/v0.23.10/app/src/main/res/drawable-nodpi).

## Preview prompt

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: a 16:9 background still for the live caption placement preview inside an Android TV app. Generate a beautiful cinematic landscape photograph: quiet mountain lake, forested slopes, distant alpine ridges, gentle dawn light and a restrained indigo-blue and warm amber color balance. Frame a clear scenic focal point in the upper half. Keep the lower third visually calm with dark water and soft reflections so real app-rendered subtitles remain legible on top. Polished natural photography, inviting and peaceful, subtle film grain, no extreme contrast. Wide landscape 16:9 composition. No text, captions, letters, numbers, logos, borders, devices, UI, or watermark. This is an actual image asset, not a mockup.

## Setup prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: welcoming artwork for the setup and home screen of Hearth, a TV speech-caption app. Create a polished soft 3D editorial illustration of a modern television and a smartphone beside it on a simple low console in a cozy uncluttered living room. The television and phone are the central subjects, with a gentle flowing ribbon of light connecting them to suggest easy phone-assisted setup and speech. Soft tactile materials, rounded forms, warm evening light, muted indigo and cream with restrained amber accents, a friendly calm mood. Wide 16:9 landscape composition that remains clear when cropped slightly on narrow layouts; center the devices with ample breathing room. Screens show only soft abstract gradients. No people, letters, text, numbers, logos, QR codes, UI buttons, captions, watermark or borders. Production-ready app artwork, not an interface mockup.
